Privacy and visitor statistics

This site uses private analytics to understand how its research pages are used.

What is collected

Umami Cloud records page paths and titles, referring pages, browser and device information, language, and approximate geographic location. URL query strings and fragments are excluded from page addresses and referrers. No advertising identifiers, session recordings, or custom personal identifiers are configured.

Umami processes IP addresses to estimate location and distinguish visits, but does not store them as fields in its standard analytics records. Its infrastructure may process or retain IP addresses in separate operational and security logs. See Umami's privacy policy and metric definitions.

How statistics are used

The analytics dashboard and detailed reports are private. Reports summarize traffic, popular pages, referrals, countries, and US states rather than publishing individual visits. Location estimates are approximate, and pageviews are not a count of people. Collection began in November 2026; earlier geographic statistics are unavailable.

The existing Hits.sh badge is a separate aggregate hit counter. It does not provide the geographic reports.

Privacy preferences

Umami analytics does not use cookies. This site's analytics filter declines recording when a browser sends an enabled Do Not Track preference or Global Privacy Control signal. Analytics blockers may also prevent recording. This does not prevent ordinary network requests or infrastructure logging needed to load the website and its resources.
