## Research narrative My publications center on the source, transport, transformation, and preservation of particulate organic carbon in river corridors. Recent work emphasizes Arctic floodplains and deltas, large Asian rivers, reservoir-regulated carbon sequestration, and data synthesis for modern river organic carbon archives. ## Featured papers

Persistent organic carbon storage in river floodplains over millennia

Ke, Y., West, A. J., Geyman, E. C., Huy, K. A., Dion-Kirschner, H., Smith, M. I., Anadu, J. S., Magyar, J. S., Lamb, M. P., & Fischer, W. W. (2026). Nature Communications. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72405-9

Shows that discontinuous permafrost floodplains can preserve organic carbon over millennial timescales, linking carbon fate to river migration, erosion, and sediment storage.

Regulation of particulate organic carbon by cascade mega-reservoirs in the Changjiang basin

Ke, Y., Calmels, D., Bouchez, J., Noret, A., Massault, M., Chetelat, B., et al. (2025). Global Biogeochemical Cycles, 39, e2024GB008479. https://doi.org/10.1029/2024GB008479

Quantifies how cascade mega-reservoirs enhance particulate organic carbon sequestration and alter downstream carbon composition in a major river basin.

MOdern River archivEs of Particulate Organic Carbon: MOREPOC

Ke, Y., Calmels, D., Bouchez, J., & Quantin, C. (2022). Earth System Science Data, 14, 4743–4755. https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-14-4743-2022

Introduces a data resource for elemental and isotopic composition of river particulate organic carbon across modern river systems.
