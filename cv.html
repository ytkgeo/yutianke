Research focus

  • Coupling Earth's surface processes and the carbon cycle from modern to geologic timescales.
  • Organic carbon preservation, degradation kinetics, and burial in floodplains and deltas.
  • Mud transport and flocculation in relation to biogeochemical cycling.
  • Carbon cycling in a changing cryosphere.
  • Carbon cycling under anthropogenic forcing.

Methods and skills

Geochemical and isotopic analysis: EA-IRMS, DI-IRMS, MICADAS AMS, ramped pyrolysis/oxidation (RPO), GasBench, XRF, and ICP-MS.

Mineralogical, microscopic, and spectroscopic analysis: XRD, NanoSIMS, SEM-EDS, Raman spectroscopy, and FT-IR.

Sediment and geotechnical characterization: Mastersizer and LS Coulter particle-size analysis, TriStar specific surface area analysis, and geotechnical testing.

Laboratory instrumentation and method development: Gas vacuum-line design, construction, and operation; wet-chemistry sample-preparation system design and fabrication; and geochemical sample pretreatment.

Field survey and instrumentation: RTK-GNSS, sonar, ADCP, UAV surveys, and LI-COR in situ flux measurements.

GIS and remote sensing: ArcGIS and Google Earth Engine.

Programming and computational tools: Python, R, and large language model-based tools.

Languages: Chinese (native), English (fluent), and French (basic).
