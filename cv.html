Research focus

  • Earth's carbon cycle and habitability across modern to geologic timescales.
  • Organic carbon preservation efficiency and degradation kinetics in floodplains and deltas.
  • Fluvial sediment transport controls on organic carbon transport, fluxes, and residence time.
  • Critical-zone hydrology, organic carbon cycling, and anthropogenic regulation.
  • Interactions between landscape evolution and biogeochemical cycles regulated by flocculation.
