About

Connecting sediment motion to the global carbon cycle

I am a geochemist and geomorphologist at Caltech. My work asks how physical landscape processes shape Earth's carbon cycle and habitability across modern to geologic timescales.

I combine field campaigns, geochemical measurements, remote sensing, and quantitative analysis to track organic carbon from upland rivers into floodplains and deltas. Current projects focus on Arctic permafrost floodplains, flocculated mud transport, reservoirs, and critical-zone hydrology.

Research

Landscapes are active parts of the carbon cycle

My research spans scales from individual sediment particles to continental river basins.

Arctic floodplains
